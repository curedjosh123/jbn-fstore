About Us

The Story Behind JBN F-Store

A little food store with a big appetite for making people happy, one order at a time.

Our Story

Started with a Simple Craving

JBN F-Store began as a small neighborhood food stall with one goal: serve genuinely good food, fast, without cutting corners. The "F" stands for Food because at the end of the day, that's all we're really about.

What started with a handful of burgers and a fryer has grown into a full menu of Filipino-favorite comfort food — from crispy fried chicken and cheesy pizza to milk tea and rice meals — all made to order, every single day.

Our Mission

Good Food, Honest Prices, Every Time

We're on a mission to make satisfying, freshly made food accessible to every craving and every budget, without ever compromising on taste or quality.

Every dish that leaves our kitchen is made with real ingredients, cooked fresh, and packed with care, because we believe fast food shouldn't mean forgettable food.

Our Vision

A Craving-Friendly Name in Every Neighborhood

We envision JBN F-Store as the go-to name for Filipino families and friends craving a satisfying meal — whether it's a quick solo burger run or a full family feast.

As we grow, our promise stays the same: fresh food, warm service, and a menu that always has something for everyone at the table.
